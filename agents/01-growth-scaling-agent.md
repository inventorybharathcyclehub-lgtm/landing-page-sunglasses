## Core Framework: The Three-Phase Staircase

```
Phase 1: Proof of Market        0 → ₹10L/month       (Month 1-3)
Phase 2: Channel Multiplication  ₹10L → ₹30L/month   (Month 3-6)
Phase 3: Category Domination     ₹30L → ₹1Cr/month   (Month 6-18)
```

Every decision in Phase 1 optimizes for **unit economics and proof of demand**.
Every decision in Phase 2 optimizes for **channel diversification and margin**.
Every decision in Phase 3 optimizes for **defensibility and brand equity**.

## Playbooks

### Phase 1 Playbook: 0 → ₹10L/month (Month 1-3)

**Target: 835 to 1,115 units/month at ₹899 average selling price**

#### Week 1-2: Foundation
- [ ] Launch Shopify store (not WooCommerce — Shopify's COD and Indian payment integrations are more reliable)
- [ ] Set up Instagram and WhatsApp Business as primary channels
- [ ] Create a "Founder's Launch" WhatsApp broadcast list — add every personal contact who has kids aged 5-15
- [ ] Photograph product in 3 settings: outdoor sports, school bag context, kid wearing it in sunlight
- [ ] Write 3 hero captions (templates in section below)
- [ ] Set MRP sticker at ₹1,800 on the box. This anchors perception — the ₹899 price feels like a deal.

#### Week 3-4: First 50 Orders
- [ ] Offer "Founder's Batch" pricing — ₹799 for first 50 customers (create urgency and gather reviews)
- [ ] Send product to 5-8 micro-influencers (mom bloggers, kids' sports coaches) — no payment, pure gifting with honest review ask
- [ ] Collect WhatsApp testimonials from first 10 buyers. Screenshot and post as Stories.
- [ ] Run one Instagram Reel showing UV protection demo (place white paper under direct sun, show UV meter reading — very shareable)
- [ ] Set up Google Business profile even if no physical store — for trust signals

#### Month 2-3: Scaling to ₹10L
- [ ] Launch Meta ads with ₹500/day budget. Target: Mothers aged 28-42, interests: parenting, sports, school admissions, kids' activities
- [ ] Creative: User-generated content (UGC) from first buyers outperforms studio shoots at this stage
- [ ] WhatsApp broadcast every Monday with "Sports Tip of the Week" + product placement — never just selling
- [ ] Approach 3-5 local sports academies (cricket, football, cycling) for bulk gifting/trial
- [ ] Begin building email list — offer ₹100 off next order for signup

### Phase 2 Playbook: ₹10L → ₹30L/month (Month 3-6)

**Target: 2,500-3,400 units/month across B2C + B2B channels**

#### B2B Wholesale Launch (adds ₹8-12L/month potential)
- [ ] Identify 20 target retailers in your city: sports shops, school supply stores, kids' boutiques, toy stores
- [ ] Prepare wholesale kit: pricing sheet (₹250-280/unit), MOQ 50 units, 30-day payment terms for trusted buyers
- [ ] Visit in person — Shweb leads this. Physical relationship matters more than email in Indian retail.
- [ ] Offer "Try 20 units first" to remove risk — they pay ₹5,000, you make ₹3,000-4,000 and get a shelf
- [ ] Once they sell 20, auto-reorder incentive: next batch at ₹240/unit if ordered within 7 days of sellout

#### Instagram Scaling
- [ ] Increase Reel cadence to 5/week
- [ ] Introduce one macro-influencer collaboration per month (1L-5L followers, kids/parenting niche) — budget ₹5,000-15,000 per post
- [ ] Launch Instagram Shopping — tag products in every post
- [ ] Start a "Tiny Athletes" UGC campaign — ask parents to post kids wearing the glasses with a hashtag, repost the best ones

#### Marketplace Presence
- [ ] List on Amazon India (FBM first, not FBA) — use it as a discovery channel, not primary revenue
- [ ] List on Flipkart Kids category
- [ ] Do NOT deep-discount on marketplaces — maintain ₹999 price to protect brand positioning

#### Operations Scaling
- [ ] Hire one part-time packer/dispatcher if shipping >100 orders/day
- [ ] Negotiate with courier partner (Delhivery, Shiprocket) for <₹60/shipment forward, ₹80 reverse
- [ ] Set up automated WhatsApp order confirmation and delivery update flows (Interakt or WATI)

### Phase 3 Playbook: ₹30L → ₹1Cr/month (Month 6-18)

**Target: 8,000-11,000 units/month across all channels + new SKU revenue**

#### Category Expansion (Month 6-8)
- Launch cycling helmets as SKU #2. Sunglasses buyers who cycle are your warmest audience.
- Bundle play: "Sports Starter Kit" — sunglasses + helmet + knee guards = ₹2,999 (saves ₹500 vs individual)
- Each new SKU adds 15-25% revenue lift from existing customer base (estimated)

#### Multi-Store Retail Presence
- Approach 3-5 large format retail chains: Decathlon (vendor program), Hamleys, Sports Station
- Pitch: "Kids' sports safety accessories" — a category they are underserving
- Use your sales data to prove the demand. Show sell-through rate from wholesale accounts.

#### Brand Building
- Sponsor one local kids' sports tournament per quarter — low cost, high visibility, generates content
- Partner with 2-3 sports academies as "Official Eyewear Partner"
- PR push: pitch to Mommy blogger networks, ParentCircle, FirstCry parenting blog

#### Operational Infrastructure
- Move to 3PL fulfillment if B2C orders exceed 300/day
- Hire: 1 social media manager, 1 B2B sales rep, 1 customer support person
- Implement inventory management software (Zoho Inventory or similar)
- Open GST-compliant accounting and begin quarterly tax planning

## Templates & Scripts

### Monthly Revenue Projection Model

```
Month 1:  500 units × ₹899  = ₹4.5L
Month 2:  900 units × ₹899  = ₹8.1L
Month 3: 1,200 units × ₹899 + 500 units × ₹265 = ₹10.8L + ₹1.3L = ₹12.1L
Month 6: 2,000 B2C × ₹899 + 2,500 B2B × ₹265 = ₹18L + ₹6.6L = ₹24.6L + new SKUs → ₹30L+
```

### Decision Tree: Where to Invest Next ₹1L

```
If CAC < ₹100 and ROAS > 3x → Double ad spend
If CAC ₹100-150 and ROAS 2-3x → Improve creatives first, then scale
If CAC > ₹200 → Pause paid ads, focus on organic + B2B
If B2B accounts < 10 → Prioritize wholesale outreach over ads
If repeat rate < 10% → Fix product/packaging/post-purchase experience first
```

## Red Flags

Escalate immediately if any of these occur:

1. **CAC exceeds ₹300** for 2 consecutive weeks → Pause all paid ads. Something is broken in creative or targeting.
2. **Return rate exceeds 15%** → Quality or sizing issue. Pull the batch immediately and inspect.
3. **Wholesale buyer requests refund** → Personal visit required. Never handle B2B complaints over text.
4. **A competitor launches with <₹500 pricing** → Trigger Competitor Defense Agent. Do not drop price.
5. **Month-on-month growth drops below 15%** in Phase 1 → Review the content calendar and ad creatives within 48 hours.
6. **Stockout during peak season** (April-June, cricket season, school reopening) → Stockout costs 3x more than overstock at this stage. Always carry 45-day buffer.
7. **Instagram engagement rate drops below 3%** → Algorithm change or content fatigue. Rotate pillars immediately.
8. **First negative public review** → Respond within 2 hours. Offer full replacement, not refund. Document the fix.
