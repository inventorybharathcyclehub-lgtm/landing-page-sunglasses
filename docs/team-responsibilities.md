# Team Responsibilities — SPORTVIO

## Overview

SPORTVIO is co-founded by **Syed** and **Shweb**. This document defines clear ownership to avoid overlap and ensure accountability at every stage.

---

## Shweb — Sourcing & Supply Chain (Front-End of the Business)

Shweb owns everything between the manufacturer and the warehouse shelf.

### Core Responsibilities

| Area | Tasks |
|------|-------|
| **Vendor Sourcing** | Find and vet manufacturers/wholesalers for sunglasses (current ₹100/unit target → ₹70–75 direct source) |
| **Sample Evaluation** | Test every new sample for UV certification, build quality, lens clarity, and durability |
| **Supplier Negotiation** | Negotiate unit price, MOQ (minimum order quantity), lead times, and payment terms |
| **Procurement** | Place and track purchase orders; ensure correct quantity, specs, and delivery timelines |
| **Inventory Management** | Monitor stock levels; trigger reorder when stock drops below 2-week threshold |
| **Quality Control (QC)** | Inspect each incoming batch — reject defective units before they reach customers |
| **Packaging Procurement** | Source premium boxes, hard cases, UV cloths, and branded inserts |
| **Future SKU Sourcing** | Source helmets, knee guards, elbow guards, knuckle guards, and cycling shorts when Phase 2 begins |

### Key Targets for Shweb
- Reduce unit cost from ₹100 to ₹70–75 within 60 days (via direct manufacturer contact)
- Maintain minimum 500-unit buffer stock at all times
- First batch: 300 units minimum, QC'd and ready within 14 days of supplier confirmation
- Packaging finalized: branded box + hard case + UV cloth — all within ₹30–35 per unit

### Syed's Role as Support to Shweb
- Syed will provide the **approved vendor list** (research-backed, verified IndiaMART/Alibaba suppliers)
- Syed will handle **payment/transfer** to suppliers (as he manages the financial backend)
- Syed will **approve final unit price** before any PO is placed

---

## Syed — Marketing & Business Backend

Syed owns everything after the product reaches the warehouse — and drives the entire demand engine.

### Core Responsibilities

| Area | Tasks |
|------|-------|
| **Landing Page & Website** | Maintain and update the SPORTVIO website (this codebase). Add products, update pricing, publish offers |
| **Instagram Content** | Plan, shoot (or brief), and post content to Instagram. Minimum 1 Reel/day in Month 1–2 |
| **Influencer Outreach** | Identify and reach out to parent micro-influencers (5K–50K followers) for gifted reviews |
| **B2B Outreach** | Call and WhatsApp sports academies, optical shops, schools — present the wholesale programme |
| **Customer Feedback Calls** | Personally call every customer after delivery to collect feedback (all feedback goes into a shared doc) |
| **Feedback Analysis** | Weekly review of feedback data — identify top 3 improvement areas, brief Shweb on product implications |
| **WhatsApp Broadcast** | Manage customer WhatsApp broadcast list — send weekly offers, restocks, and announcements |
| **Order Operations** | Receive orders, brief Shweb for dispatch, confirm delivery, handle returns/exchanges |
| **Finance Tracking** | Track unit economics (cost, revenue, margin) on a shared spreadsheet — update after every B2B deal |
| **Consulting Agent Use** | Use the AI consulting agents in /agents for strategy decisions — document outcomes |

### Key Targets for Syed
- **Month 1:** Landing page live. 30 Instagram posts. 5 B2B leads contacted.
- **Month 2:** ₹3–5L revenue. 10 B2B orders placed. 50 customer feedback calls done.
- **Month 3:** ₹8–10L revenue. 3 academy partnerships. Influencer reviews live.

## Shared Responsibilities

| Task | Syed | Shweb |
|------|------|-------|
| Weekly business review call | ✓ Leads | ✓ Participates |
| New product decisions | ✓ Approves market fit | ✓ Approves sourcing viability |
| Pricing decisions | ✓ Owns final call | ✓ Input on cost floor |
| Customer complaints (product defects) | ✓ Customer face | ✓ Resolves at source |
| Capital allocation decisions | ✓ Both agree | ✓ Both agree |

---

## Communication Norms

- **Daily update**: Each person sends a 2-line WhatsApp update each evening — what they did, what's blocked.
- **Weekly review**: Every Monday, 30-minute call to review revenue, stock, feedback, and next week's plan.
- **Escalation rule**: If a decision affects >₹10,000 in spend or a major supplier relationship, both founders align before acting.
