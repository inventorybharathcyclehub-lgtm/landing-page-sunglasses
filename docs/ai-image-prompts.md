# SPORTVIO — AI Image Generation Prompts
## Research-Backed · Dual-Audience · Indian Context

These prompts are built from deep analysis of POC Kids, Babiators, Grech & Co, Patagonia Kids, Oakley Kids, Banz, Lovevery, and Roshambo photography. The core principle across all premium kids brands: **the child is the hero in every visual — active, joyful, in motion — the copy speaks to the parent, the image speaks to the child.**

### How to Use
- **Midjourney v6**: Paste prompt after `/imagine`. Add `--ar 16:9` for website banners, `--ar 4:5` for Instagram.
- **DALL-E 3**: Paste directly. Add "photorealistic, not illustrated" if needed.
- **Adobe Firefly**: Use "Photo" mode, not "Art" mode.
- **Stability AI**: Works with the full prompt as-is.

### Non-Negotiable Rules for Every Image
- Indian child: brown skin, South Asian facial features
- Child is IN MOTION or in a natural documentary moment — never posed for the camera
- Sunglasses are ON the child's face during the activity — never isolated product shots
- Lighting: warm, natural — golden hour or bright Indian midday sun
- Avoid: studio backgrounds, cold/blue tones, stock photo aesthetic, Western settings

### Image Composition Principles from Brand Research
1. **Low angle** — shoot upward at the child to make them appear heroic, not small
2. **Natural light only** — golden hour or bright midday sun; no flash, no studio
3. **Motion blur on background** — child sharp, world blurred = sense of speed and energy
4. **Real kids not models** — freckles, messy hair, scraped knees, grass stains are premium
5. **Child as independent actor** — the child is leading, deciding, moving — not being directed by a parent
6. **Product on face during activity** — never floating or isolated; always worn, always in use
